# Talenfra — Three Short-Form Scripts (from the Senior PM demo)

Shared facts (locked): 47 applicants, one "Senior Product Manager" role.
Runs in "a couple of minutes." Saves "nearly five hours" (card reads 4.7 — never round past that).
Differentiator: it shows the exact résumé line behind every score. Rank, don't reject — a human keeps the final call.
One CTA everywhere: **book a free audit — we'll run it on one of your real roles.**

---

## 1) 30-Second Paid Ad — Meta / YouTube in-feed (cold traffic)

### Hook options (first 3 seconds)
- **A (mechanism):** "This tool read 47 CVs in the time it takes to make coffee — and it can show you the exact line behind every score."
- **B (proof-on-screen):** "Every score you're about to see comes with the résumé line that earned it. No black box."
- **C (pain-to-relief):** "Screening one role's CVs eats a recruiter's afternoon. Watch it take a couple of minutes."

Recommended: **A** for cold traffic (motion + specific number + the differentiator in one breath). B is the strongest for a skeptical recruiting audience if you're retargeting.

### Full 30s script (~74 words VO)

| Time | [SCREEN] | VO / Caption |
|------|----------|--------------|
| 0–3s | Rubric criteria filled in, cursor hovering **Run**. | "This read 47 CVs for one role — in a couple of minutes." |
| 3–8s | Click Run → live scoring animation ticking through applicants. | "You set the criteria. It scores every CV, 0 to 100." |
| 8–14s | Ranked table resolves: scores, tiers A-B-C, actions. | "You get a ranked shortlist — not a delete button. A recruiter still makes the call." |
| 14–21s | Click a row → criterion scores expand, each next to the exact résumé line. | "And here's the part that matters: the exact line from the CV behind every score." |
| 21–26s | "What it caught" panel: unexplained gap, 4 job changes in 2 years. | "It flags what's easy to miss — gaps, job-hopping, skills never actually used." |
| 26–30s | ROI card "≈4.7 hours saved" → end card: talenfra.com. | "Nearly five hours saved on one role. Book a free audit — we'll run it on one of yours." |

**Why this hook/structure:** Leads on the single most characteristic thing (speed + shows-its-work) instead of pain the buyer already knows. Mechanism → the anti-black-box proof → the fear-defuser ("not a delete button") → payoff number → one CTA. Nothing splits the ask.

## 2) 60–90s Homepage Cut-Down (warm — already on the site)

Target ~80s. Viewer chose to watch, so we can breathe a little, but we cut privacy detail and half the red flags.

| Time | [SCREEN] | VO |
|------|----------|-----|
| 0–6s | Role header "Senior Product Manager · 47 applicants", rubric visible. | "Here's one role, 47 CVs. Normally that's a recruiter's afternoon gone." |
| 6–12s | Rubric criteria in view, cursor to **Run**. | "You give it the role and your criteria — the same things you'd screen for by hand." |
| 12–20s | Click Run → live scoring animation. | "It reads every CV and scores each one, 0 to 100, against your rubric. This takes a couple of minutes." |
| 20–30s | ROI card "≈4.7 hours saved" → ranked table: score / tier / action. | "That's nearly five hours back. You get a ranked shortlist with a tier and a suggested action for each." |
| 30–40s | Point at a 'reject' recommendation row. | "And 'reject' is only a recommendation — never automatic. Borderline candidates get flagged, nobody's binned without a human looking." |
| 40–58s | Click a row → criteria expand, each beside the exact résumé line. | "Now the part most tools skip. Click any candidate and you see every score with the exact line from their CV behind it. So when a client asks why someone made your shortlist, you can point at the evidence. No black box." |
| 58–70s | "What it caught" panel — hold on one flag: 4 job changes in 2 years. | "It also surfaces what's easy to miss — like four job changes in two years — so nothing slips through at speed." |
| 70–80s | Export-to-CSV click → end frame talenfra.com. | "Export the shortlist in a click. Want to see it on your own hiring? Book a free audit — we'll run it on one of your real roles, so you see exactly what it saves." |

**Why this structure:** Keeps the two beats that actually sell — the evidence/"defend it to a client" moment and one concrete red flag — and trims proof they don't need again on-site. The 'reject is only a recommendation' line stays because it's the buyer's core fear. Single CTA, framed as their real role, not "learn more."

## 3) LinkedIn Post + Native-Video Hook (aimed at recruiting-firm owners)

### Video hook (3–5s, spoken + on-screen text)
> "You know that shortlist you sent a client last week? Could you show them the line from each CV behind it?"

(On-screen text mirrors the spoken line; cut straight into the row-expand footage showing scores next to résumé lines.)

### The post (~140 words)

> Your recruiters lose the best part of a day screening CVs for a single role.
>
> And when a client pushes back on a shortlist — "why this candidate, not that one?" — the honest answer is usually a gut feel you can't fully show.
>
> We build the thing that fixes both.
>
> You give it a role and your criteria. It reads every CV, scores each one 0–100 against your rubric, and hands back a ranked shortlist — in a couple of minutes.
>
> Two things we're strict about:
>
> — It ranks, it doesn't reject. A recruiter always makes the final call. Nobody gets auto-binned.
> — Every score comes with the exact line from the CV behind it. So you can defend a shortlist to a client with evidence, not a hunch.
>
> On one role it saved nearly five hours.
>
> Want to see it on one of your real roles? We'll run a free audit — comment or DM and we'll set it up.
>
> #recruitment #staffing

**Why this hook/structure:** Opens on the owner's world — the client challenge they can't fully answer — which lands harder than "save time" for someone who runs the firm. The two "strict about" bullets do the fear-defusing (not a black box, not auto-reject) in their language. One CTA, low-friction (comment/DM for the audit).

## Flags to verify before publishing
1. **"Nearly five hours" / 4.7:** true only if that's the number your ROI card actually shows for this run. Keep the card visible in the ad so the claim matches the frame. The figure is computed (CVs × minutes-per-CV) — be ready to explain the basis.
2. **"A couple of minutes":** make sure the recorded run reads as ~2 min, not longer, or trim the scoring-animation footage to match.
3. **LinkedIn CTA mechanics:** confirm you're happy fielding "comment or DM" (vs. a link). If you'd rather send to talenfra.com, swap the last line — but keep it to one ask.
4. **Red-flag examples** (unexplained gap, 4 job changes in 2 years, skills never used): only use the ones your "What it caught" panel genuinely displays for this run.
