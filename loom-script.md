# Talenfra — Loom Demo Script (final, ~2:25)

**Format:** screen recording of the demo + voiceover. No face-cam. Calm and plain — a walkthrough, not a hype reel.
**Reviewed** by a marketing pass and revised (see "What changed" below).
**On-screen data is synthetic.** Role: Senior Product Manager, 47 applicants.

---

### 0:00 – 0:10 · Hook
**SCREEN:** Demo setup screen — "Senior Product Manager · 47 applications."
**VO:** "Screening 47 CVs for one role eats a recruiter's afternoon. Our system does it in about two minutes — and shows the exact line from each CV behind every score. Watch."

### 0:10 – 0:28 · What it scores against
**SCREEN:** Point at the rubric chips (SaaS background, domain fit, leadership, communication, tenure) and the knockout.
**VO:** "First we tell it what good looks like for your role — your real criteria, not keywords. Here, five things that matter for a Senior PM, plus one hard rule: at least four years in product."

### 0:28 – 0:48 · Run it
**SCREEN:** Click **Run screening**. Progress bar fills, shortlist/hold/reject tallies climb.
**VO:** "Now it reads every CV and scores each one against that rubric — sorting into shortlist, hold, or reject as it goes. What takes a recruiter an afternoon finishes in a couple of minutes."

### 0:48 – 1:05 · The payoff up top
**SCREEN:** Results load — ROI card at the top (~4.7 hrs).
**VO:** "47 people screened, and nearly five hours of manual work handed back to your team. But speed isn't the real point — trust is. So here's what it actually gives you."

### 1:05 – 1:28 · Ranked, never auto-binned
**SCREEN:** The ranked table — point at scores, tiers, actions.
**VO:** "Every candidate ranked — score, tier, and a recommended action. And 'reject' here is only a recommendation, never automatic. Borderline people get flagged for a human to look at. Your team always makes the final call — nobody's binned by a machine."

### 1:28 – 1:52 · Every score shows its work  ← the money beat
**SCREEN:** Click a row to expand → per-criterion bars + the résumé lines behind each score.
**VO:** "This is the part that matters. Click any candidate and you see exactly why they scored the way they did — the line from their own CV behind every number. No black box. If you'd score it differently, you can see what it read and make the call yourself."

### 1:52 – 2:10 · What it caught
**SCREEN:** Scroll to the "What it caught" panel.
**VO:** "It also surfaces what's easy to miss on a fast read — an unexplained gap, four job changes in two years, skills listed but never actually used. Flagged for you, not buried at the bottom of the pile."

### 2:10 – 2:28 · The deliverable + privacy
**SCREEN:** Click **Export shortlist** → CSV toast appears.
**VO:** "When you're happy, the shortlist is one click away — ranked and annotated, ready to drop into your spreadsheet or send straight to the client. It runs on your data, kept confidential — nothing's used to train public models."

### 2:28 – 2:45 · Close (one CTA)
**SCREEN:** The ROI / next-step card, or cut to talenfra.com.
**VO:** "That's Talenfra. We build this around your roles and your criteria — you keep the final call, we take the volume off your plate. Book a free audit and we'll run it on one of your real roles, so you see exactly what it saves. This demo used made-up data — on your roles, it works exactly the same way."

## What changed in the marketing review
- **Hook** now leads with the differentiator ("shows the line behind every score") instead of conditional pain — stops a skeptical recruiter faster.
- **"about five hours" → "nearly five hours"** so the voiceover doesn't round *up* past the on-screen 4.7 (a skeptic notices).
- **Reconciled the word "reject"** on screen with the "nothing's binned" promise, out loud — this is the exact black-box fear to defuse.
- **One time unit** ("an afternoon") held throughout; one hard number ("nearly five hours") only on the ROI beat.
- **Added two buyer beats:** where the output goes (spreadsheet / client) and **data privacy** (not used to train public models).
- **Concrete CTA:** "we'll run it on one of your real roles" (that *is* the free audit) instead of a vague "we'll show you."
- Trimmed ~35 seconds (merged the two "it's running" beats, shortened the ROI transition). Now ~2:25.

## Decisions for you (30 seconds)
1. **ATS line.** The reviewer wanted "drop into your ATS." The demo exports a plain CSV, and clean ATS import is a paid-build feature — so I kept the honest version ("drop into your spreadsheet or send straight to the client"). If you can genuinely import into Bullhorn/JobAdder/Vincere, say the word and I'll switch it to "…drop into your ATS."
2. **Role name.** Script uses **Senior Product Manager** (matches the demo). Your homepage `DemoSection` still says "Business Development Manager" — pick one and I'll align the site copy.
3. **Time framing.** Script says "a couple of minutes / an afternoon," not the site's "28 minutes." Worth updating the site line to match.

## Recording tips
- Pace slow. Pause a beat after each on-screen click so the viewer's eye catches up.
- Practice the run once — know when the animation ends so your VO lands on the results.
- Record VO and screen together in Loom, or do a silent screen pass and voice over it after (cleaner).
- If you want it under 2:00, cut the "What it caught" beat — but it's a strong trust moment; keep it if you can.
