# Talenfra — Loom Demo Script (~2:25)

**Format:** screen recording of a live screening run + voiceover. No face-cam. Calm and plain — a walkthrough, not a hype reel.
Role: Senior Product Manager, 47 applicants.

---

### 0:00 – 0:10 · Hook (differentiator-first)
**SCREEN:** Ranked results flash up, then settle on the top of the table — "Senior Product Manager · 47 applicants."
**VO:** "on an average day your recruiters would spend approximately 5 hours to review 47 applications for one application but using this tool its only a matter of minutes, to get a detailed cleaning of each and every applicant, with scores identifying the best fit. We are not talking about replacing recruiters, but I'm empowering them with something that enhances their capabilities, let's watch, how."

### 0:10 – 0:28 · What it scores against
**SCREEN:** Point at the rubric chips (SaaS background, domain fit, leadership, communication, tenure) and the knockout rule.
**VO:** "First we tell it what good looks like for your role — your real criteria, not keywords. Here, five things that matter for a Senior PM, plus one hard rule: at least four years in product."

### 0:28 – 0:48 · Run it
**SCREEN:** Click **Run screening**. Progress bar fills, shortlist/hold/reject tallies climb.
**VO:** "Now it reads every CV and scores each one against that rubric — sorting into shortlist, hold, or reject as it goes. What takes a recruiter an afternoon finishes in a couple of minutes."

### 0:48 – 1:05 · The payoff up top
**SCREEN:** Results load — ROI card at the top (~4.7 hrs).
**VO:** "47 people screened, and nearly five hours your team didn't spend reading CVs. But speed isn't the real point — trust is. So here's what it actually gives you."

### 1:05 – 1:28 · Ranked, never auto-binned
**SCREEN:** The ranked table — point at scores, tiers, actions.
**VO:** "Every candidate ranked — score, tier, and a recommended action. And 'reject' here is only a recommendation, never automatic. Borderline people get flagged for a human to look at. Your team always makes the final call — nobody's binned by a machine."

### 1:28 – 1:52 · Every score shows its work  ← the money beat
**SCREEN:** Click a row to expand → per-criterion bars + the résumé lines behind each score.
**VO:** "This is the part that matters. Click any candidate and you see exactly why they scored the way they did — the line from their own CV behind every score. No black box. So when a client asks why someone made your shortlist, you point at the evidence — not a hunch."

### 1:52 – 2:10 · What it caught
**SCREEN:** Scroll to the "What it caught" panel.
**VO:** "It also surfaces what's easy to miss on a fast read — an unexplained gap, four job changes in two years, skills listed but never actually used. Flagged for you to weigh, not binned."

### 2:10 – 2:28 · The deliverable + privacy
**SCREEN:** Click **Export shortlist** → CSV toast appears.
**VO:** "When you're happy, the shortlist is one click away — ranked and annotated, ready to drop into your spreadsheet or send straight to the client. It runs on your data, kept confidential — nothing's used to train public models."

### 2:28 – 2:45 · Close (one CTA, last)
**SCREEN:** The ROI / next-step card, or cut to talenfra.com.
**VO:** "That's Talenfra — a real screening run, start to finish. We build it around your roles and your criteria, you keep the final call, we take the volume off your plate. Book a free audit and we'll run it on one of your real roles, so you see exactly what it saves."

---

## Why these choices
- **Hook** leads with the differentiator ("the line behind every score") + a real result — stops a skeptic faster than pain they already feel.
- **Structure** holds where it works: rubric → run → ROI → rank-don't-reject → evidence beat → red flags → deliverable → CTA.
- **Fear-defusers** ("only a recommendation," "final call," "nobody's binned") stay verbatim — that's the black-box fear.
- **One CTA, and it lands last** (free audit on one of their real roles). No disclaimer after the ask.
- **"A couple of minutes"** and **"nearly five hours"** held throughout — one time unit, one number, both matching what's on screen.

## Two things to have an answer ready for
- **"Nearly five hours" (4.7)** is computed from CVs × minutes-per-CV — be ready to say the basis if asked. Keep the ROI card in frame so the claim matches the visual.
- **Red-flag examples** — only narrate the ones your "What it caught" panel actually shows for the run you record.

## Recording tips
- Pace slow. Pause a beat after each on-screen click so the viewer's eye catches up.
- **Hook — "Watch."** Land it flat and confident, then let the first click happen in silence.
- **The pivot — "But speed isn't the real point — trust is."** Pause on the dash, drop the pitch slightly, hit "trust" clean. Your best line.
- **Money beat — "This is the part that matters."** Slow right down, pause a full beat while the row expands so the eye catches the résumé lines before you speak. Then "No black box." — clipped, no rising inflection.
- Record VO and screen together, or do a silent screen pass and voice over it after (cleaner).
